# Meera — transition reels (plan, 18 Nov)

How we make them: two ChatGPT keyframes with the SAME framing, pose and camera height (look A, look B), then
either (1) Kling start+end frame (first/last frame) so Kling animates the change itself, or (2) two separate
Kling clips cut on a "trigger" (hand over lens, spin, hair flip, jump, snap) that I hide in the edit with a
whip blur / flash / match cut on the beat. Each reel 8-12 s, 9:16, beat-synced to the user's track.
