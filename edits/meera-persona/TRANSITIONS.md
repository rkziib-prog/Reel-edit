# Meera — transition reels (plan, 18 Nov)

How we make them: two ChatGPT keyframes with the SAME framing, pose and camera height (look A, look B), then
either (1) Kling start+end frame (first/last frame) so Kling animates the change itself, or (2) two separate
Kling clips cut on a "trigger" (hand over lens, spin, hair flip, jump, snap) that I hide in the edit with a
whip blur / flash / match cut on the beat. Each reel 8-12 s, 9:16, beat-synced to the user's track.

| # | Idea | Look A -> Look B | Trigger | Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Casual to Onam | black tee + jeans at home -> kasavu saree, jasmine, bindi, temple lamp | hand covers lens | 2 clips + cut |
| T2 | Spin change | white kurta -> emerald halter dress (Post 03 look) | 360 spin | Kling start+end frame |
| T3 | Day to night | Cubbon Park sundress, sunlight -> same spot, black satin dress, night lights | hair flip | 2 clips + match cut |
| T4 | Mirror snap | gym set at mirror -> red saree at same mirror | finger snap | 2 clips + flash |
| T5 | Kochi to Bangalore | Fort Kochi beach linen (Post 01) -> Bangalore rooftop blazer | walks toward lens, fills frame | 2 clips + whip |

Rules: identity lock + Avoid line in every keyframe; same camera distance/angle in A and B; Kling 5 s each,
9:16 set explicitly; no Kling spend without user OK.
