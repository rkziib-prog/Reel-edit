## 4. Camera movement and motion

- **Camera:** locked-off extreme close-up at eye level; no push, no drift, no handheld. The plan's stillness here is
  the point: only the face moves.
- **Motion timeline inside the 3 s used** (from the 5 s clip): 0–0.4 s still, fists pressed, mouth trembling →
  0.4–0.8 s one involuntary twitch → 0.8–2.6 s the corners of the mouth rise slowly into the closed-lip smile; tears
  keep running; eyes stay wide and desperate → 2.6–3.0 s hold the forced smile. Cut on the hold to 7.5.
- **Expression rule:** the smile is B5_v2's shape, closed lips, no teeth. The eyes never join it; that conflict is
  the shot.
